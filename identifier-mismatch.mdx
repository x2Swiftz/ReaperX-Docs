### ❓ What is this?

If you change your executor or switch devices, the script may show an **Identifier Mismatch**. This is because each key is limited to one device.

<img
  src="/images/image-6.png"
  alt="Image"
  className="mr-auto"
  style={{ width:"67%" }}
/>

### ❔ How to Solve

Press button 🔴🔒 Reset HWID

<img
  src="/images/image-16.png"
  alt="Image"
  title="Image"
  style={{ width:"64%" }}
/>

<Warning>
  If you proceed, you will use 1 Reset Token. By default, you have 2 tokens. If you need more, you will need to purchase additional Reset Tokens.
</Warning>
