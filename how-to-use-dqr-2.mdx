### (Lv. 185) - Northern Lands

For **Nightmare**, you’ll need a really strong build with high damage. Of course, our **Auto Dodge isn’t 100% perfect yet**, so you might still die 2-5 times. 

![Screenshot 2026 09 04 043941](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-04-043941.png "Screenshot 2026 09 04 043941")

<Info>
  The faster you can kill the enemies, the better
</Info>

For **Mage Class**, I recommend these skills:

- **Lightning Burst (Lv. 175)**
- **Geyser (Lv. 185)**
