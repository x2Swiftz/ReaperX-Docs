## ✅ How to use Auto Farm Raids

Using Auto Raid is very simple it works almost the same as Auto Join Mission.

The only additional feature is `Auto Open Chests`.

Simply enable:

- `Free Chests`
- `Premium Chests`

The script will automatically open available Raid chests for you.

⚠️ If the chests are not opening, it usually means you do not have the required keys.

## 🚫 Why Can’t I Join Raids?

- Your account does not meet the **Raid requirements**
- The required difficulty has **not been unlocked yet**
- Your account may be **Shadow Banned**
- Your executor may **not be fully supported**

## 💀 Why Am I Dying On Colossal?

You should upgrade your `HP and HP Regeneration` **skill trees**.

The Colossal Boss applies burn damage continuously, so low HP builds may die **very quickly.**

**It is also recommended to use:**

- Perks
- Memory Cards
- Artifacts
