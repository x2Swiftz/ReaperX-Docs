## 📝 Need Help?

Before contacting us, please read and understand the following information first.

### 📚 Important - Read Before Contacting ReaperX Support

This support system is only intended for **general assistance and customer support** related to ReaperX products.

If you need help regarding Xenon Hub, please contact the Xenon Hub Discord server instead.

### 💬 Recommended Contact Method

For faster responses, we highly recommend asking your questions in the `#❓questions`

You will usually receive assistance much faster there.

<Warning>
  We do not provide support through direct messages (DMs).\
  Please contact us only through the official support system
</Warning>
