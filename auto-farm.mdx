### ✅ Start From Level 1

Create a Dungeon to farm by choosing the **Dungeon** and **Difficulty** you can handle 

If you can't finish it before time runs out, I recommend farming the previous Dungeon first to level up

### ⚙️ After Everything Is Ready

Go to the `Auto Farm` tab and enable:

- `Auto Farm`
- `Auto Dodge`
- `Auto Attack`
- `Auto Skills`

Our Auto Farm works better with the Mage Class than Warrior. While dodging skills, Mage can keep casting skills from far away. Warrior needs to get close to the enemies for their skills to hit

<Warning>
  To make sure it keeps farming, don't forget to turn on **Auto Replay** so it will repeat the Dungeon

  If you're worried about your inventory getting full, you can also turn on **Auto Sell** and **Auto Back to Lobby**

  For example, if you set **Back to Lobby After 5 Times**, it will farm the Dungeon 5 times first, then go back to the lobby and sell your items automatically
</Warning>
