### [Lv. 185] - Northern Lands

For **Nightmare**, you'll need a strong build with enough damage to **2–3 cycle** the normal mobs

<Warning>
  Hardcore Mode is currently not supported!
</Warning>

You can choose whether you want to use **Auto Dodge Odin** or **Auto Dodge Bob**

For **Bob Mode**, you can choose:

- **Full** → It’ll dodge everything, including the Orb
- **Semi** → It’ll still dodge everything like normal, but it won’t dodge the Orb
- **Disabled Auto Dodge Orb** → It won’t dodge at all

<Check>
  I recommend using **Semi**, but it’s up to you which one you wanna use
</Check>
