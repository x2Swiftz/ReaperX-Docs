Our **Auto Sell** will sell items based on the **Rarity** you choose

**For example**, if you select **Epic**, it will sell all **Epic** items, as well as lower rarities like **Uncommon** and **Common**

For **Category**, you can choose which type of items you want to sell. The options are **Weapon, Skills, Armor, and Helmet**
